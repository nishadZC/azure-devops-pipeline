Smart Daily Ride Sharing

Share your existing commute with others traveling the same route. Save money, reduce traffic, and build a sustainable commuting community.

Key Features

Daily Route Registration

Drivers can register recurring routes, timings, and available seats. Example: Home → Office every weekday at 8:30 AM.

Ride Matching

Find nearby commuters traveling the same direction and book instantly.

Monthly Subscription

Subscribe to fixed routes for daily office or college travel at affordable monthly pricing.

Low Cost

Drivers already travel that route, reducing platform costs and making rides cheaper.
